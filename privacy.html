Privacy Policy for Arelo

Effective Date: 16 May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Arelo (“Arelo”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). Arelo is an AI-powered productivity and task management assistant designed to help users organise tasks, prioritise responsibilities, and reduce overwhelm across work and personal life.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Arelo platform, website, and related services.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Google account profile information
  • Authentication tokens required for secure login and connected account access

User Content

  • Tasks
  • Notes
  • Messages
  • Preferences
  • Productivity settings
  • Organisational categories

Email Integration Data

If you connect your Gmail or Outlook account, Arelo may access email data such as:

  • Email subject lines
  • Email sender information
  • Email snippets or message content
  • Email timestamps

This access is used solely to identify actionable tasks, reminders, follow-ups, and productivity insights.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Authenticate users securely
  • Analyse emails for actionable tasks
  • Generate AI-powered productivity insights
  • Personalise your dashboard and task prioritisation
  • Improve Arelo functionality and performance
  • Maintain platform security and prevent abuse

4. AI Processing

Arelo uses artificial intelligence to help organise and prioritise information. Your data may be processed by AI systems to detect tasks in emails, categorise responsibilities, summarise workload, generate productivity suggestions, and improve user experience.

We do not sell your personal data or email content.

5. Gmail and Google API Data Usage

Arelo’s use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Arelo only accesses Gmail data after explicit user consent.
  • Gmail data is used solely to provide productivity and task-management features.
  • Gmail data is not sold to third parties.
  • Gmail data is not used for advertising purposes.
  • Humans do not read your Gmail content unless explicitly authorised for support purposes.
  • Data access is limited to the minimum scopes required for functionality.

6. Data Storage and Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect user data, including secure authentication, encrypted connections, access controls, and secure cloud infrastructure.

Despite our efforts, no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-Party Services

Arelo may use trusted third-party services including Google OAuth, Supabase, OpenAI APIs, Make.com automation services, and cloud hosting providers.

8. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, or object to certain processing of your personal data. You may also withdraw consent where applicable.

10. Children’s Privacy

Arelo is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
