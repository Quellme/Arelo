# Privacy Policy for Arelo **Effective Date:** 16 May 2026 ## 1. Introduction Welcome to Arelo (“Arelo”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). Arelo is an AI-powered productivity and task management assistant designed to help users organise tasks, prioritise responsibilities, and reduce overwhelm across work and personal life. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Arelo platform, website, and related services. --- # 2. Information We Collect We may collect the following categories of information: ## Account Information When you create an account, we may collect: * Name * Email address * Google account profile information * Authentication tokens required for secure login ## User Content When using Arelo, you may provide: * Tasks * Notes * Messages * Preferences * Productivity settings * Organisational categories ## Email Integration Data If you connect your Gmail or Outlook account, Arelo may access: * Email subject lines * Email sender information * Email snippets or message content * Email timestamps This access is used solely to identify actionable tasks, reminders, follow-ups, and productivity insights. --- # 3. How We Use Your Information We use collected information to: * Create and manage your account * Authenticate users securely * Analyse emails for actionable tasks * Generate AI-powered productivity insights * Personalise your dashboard and task prioritisation * Improve Arelo functionality and performance * Maintain platform security and prevent abuse --- # 4. AI Processing Arelo uses artificial intelligence to help organise and prioritise information. Your data may be processed by AI systems to: * Detect tasks in emails * Categorise responsibilities * Summarise workload * Generate productivity suggestions * Improve user experience We do not sell your personal data or email content. --- # 5. Gmail and Google API Data Usage Arelo’s use of information received from Google APIs complies with the: * Google API Services User Data Policy * Limited Use requirements Specifically: * Arelo only accesses Gmail data after explicit user consent. * Gmail data is used solely to provide productivity and task-management features. * Gmail data is not sold to third parties. * Gmail data is not used for advertising purposes. * Humans do not read your Gmail content unless explicitly authorised for support purposes. * Data access is limited to the minimum scopes required for functionality. --- # 6. Data Storage and Security We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect user data, including: * Secure authentication * Encrypted connections (HTTPS) * Access controls * Secure cloud infrastructure Despite our efforts, no system can guarantee absolute security. --- # 7. Third-Party Services Arelo may use trusted third-party services including: * Google OAuth * Supabase * OpenAI APIs * Make.com automation services * Cloud hosting providers These services may process data on our behalf under their own privacy and security policies. --- # 8.
