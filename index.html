Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't

Built for people who feel overwhelmed — not people who love productivity systems.

📬
Understands what needs holding

An email, a message, something you mention — Arelo recognises what's worth holding onto, not just what's in the subject line. Each one becomes a Pin Arelo keeps track of for you.

🗂️
Organised around your life

Choose the parts of life you want Arelo to help with — Arelo organises around those, not a fixed list, so you see what's relevant right now, not everything at once.

💭
Tell Arelo anything

Got things spinning in your head? Just type or speak them. Arelo captures, categorises, and files them so you don't have to hold it all yourself.

🎯
Urgency calibrated to you

Your sensitivity to deadlines is different from everyone else's. Arelo adapts how it surfaces things — and never floods you with noise.

💬
AI companion

A calm, supportive chat assistant who knows your whole task list — email-sourced and manual — and helps you decide where to start without judgment.

🔄
Keeps up as things change

Plans move, emails arrive, things get resolved. Arelo is built to fold new information in and gently update what it's tracking — see it in the interactive demo.

🌙
Quiet hours

Set times you don't want to be disturbed. Arelo holds things back until you're ready — no pings in evenings, weekends, or whenever you need a break.
